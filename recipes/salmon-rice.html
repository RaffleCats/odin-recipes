Crispy Honey Soy Salmon

Description

This Honey Soy Salmon recipe produces 4 portions that can be served with rice, noodles, or pasta.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white rice
  • 4 salmon fillets (skin removed or on)
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 3cm piece fresh ginger (peeled and finely grated)
  • 2 cloves garlic (crushed)
  • 1 tbsp salt (rock salt)
  • Sliced green onions and sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Prepare Salmon: Take the salmon fillets out of the packet and wash with fresh water. Pat dry with paper towel and cover in 1 tbsp of rock salt. Leave to the side.
  2. Make the Glaze: Combine honey, soy sauce, grated ginger, and crushed garlic into a small bowl and mix well.
  3. Marinate: Place the salmon fillets in a shallow dish, and combine with prepared glaze. Leave to sit for 10 minutes before moving onto the next step.
  4. Cook Salmon: Place the marinated salmon fillets in a tray and bake in an oven (180C) for 20 minutes. Once complete turn off the oven and let the salmon finish cooking while completing the next step.
  5. Prepare Rice: Prepare 1 cup of white rice.
  6. Serve: Take out the salmon fillets and serve on top of rice. Use extra green onions and sesame seeds to garnish and serve.
