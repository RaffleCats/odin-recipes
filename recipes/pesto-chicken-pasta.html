Pesto Chicken Pasta

Description

A simple, spicy pesto pasta dish that can be adjusted to anyone's tastes.

The recipe requires 15mins of prep time, and 25mins of cook time. serving 8 portions total.

Ingredients

  • 16 ounces of bow-tie pasta
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp hot chile paste
  • 1 lbs boneless chicken breast
  • 6 tbsp basil pesto
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Place bow-tie pasta in the pot, cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until al dente, and drain.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Mix in the chile paste and chicken. Cook and stir chicken 10 minutes, or until evenly browned and juices run clear.
  3. Toss the cooked bow-tie pasta, pesto, Parmesan cheese, and cilantro into the skillet, and continue cooking just until heated through.
